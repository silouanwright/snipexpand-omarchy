# Product slice

A calm, keyboard-first control panel that emphasizes daemon health and fast
snippet retrieval through a compact status hero and searchable list, while
inheriting Omarchy's native popup, spacing, focus, and theme system.

The external SnipExpand daemon and CLI remain authoritative. The panel retains
snippet text only while open, edits generated snippets through the public CLI,
and opens handwritten YAML in the configured editor.
